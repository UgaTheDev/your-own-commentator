Token changes, justified
• Team chip ink is pure #ffffff, not text #e6edf3: pink with #e6edf3 is only 4.1:1, with white 4.8:1.
• Green moves #1f8a4c → #1a7d44: white on the old green is 4.4:1 (fails AA), the new one is 5.2:1 and still reads as green.
• New accentInk #070b10 for anything on yellow (13.7:1), and glass rgba(9,14,20,.88) + 24 px blur so overlay text stays AA even if blur is unavailable.
• Yellow is reserved for focus, live state and the one primary action per screen. Team colours are never decoration: they only ever mean a team.
