Your Own Commentator

One game, a commentator for everyone. New fans get the rules explained the moment they matter, kids get the hype, fantasy players get their numbers, and families hear the game in their own language. Built for Fire TV on Vega OS.

Fantasy

The rule that makes it trustworthy

No invented facts. A commentator may only say what is in the verified event and game-state data, plus a rules card we wrote by hand. Every line passes two gates before it is voiced: a code check that rejects any number not in the facts (in any script, including Devanagari and full-width digits), and an Amazon Nova judge that rejects invented names, stats or claims. A line that fails twice is dropped, not guessed.

How it works

  1. See the gameAmazon Nova Pro proposes events from the video in 20-second windows. Scoring plays are anchored to the broadcast's own score graphic, which Nova reads as an image.
  2. VerifyA human review screen accepts, fixes or adds events. The game state (score, fouls, fantasy points) is computed deterministically and must match the known final score.
  3. WriteAmazon Nova writes one line per play per commentator, sized to the gap before the next play, then passes both grounding gates.
  4. Voice and mixAmazon Polly voices each line (a different voice per persona and language), and the crowd audio ducks under the commentary.
  5. Watch on Fire TVThe Vega OS app plays the game with your commentator, shows rule cards on cue, and lets you switch commentators with the remote.

Why it matters

Amateur, school and college games have no commentary at all, and one broadcast track has to serve every viewer of a big game. A commentator made for you makes live sports easier to follow for new fans, kids and families who speak another language.
