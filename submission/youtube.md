## Title
Your Own Commentator: pick your own sports commentator on Fire TV

## Description
Your Own Commentator is a Fire TV app for Vega OS. Pick a game, then pick who calls it: a Rookie who explains every rule, a Kid, a two-voice Booth, a Fantasy analyst, or the Rookie in Spanish, Hindi or Mandarin. Switch voices mid-play with the remote. Ask "why?" from the couch, or out loud from your phone, and the commentator answers about that play. Every spoken line is checked in code against the verified play log before it is voiced.

Recorded on the Vega Virtual Device (the Fire TV / Vega OS simulator). Built for the Amazon Developer Hackathon 2026, Fire TV track.

Built with Amazon Nova Pro, Nova 2 Lite and Nova 2 Sonic (Amazon Bedrock), Amazon Polly, Amazon ECS on Fargate, S3 and CloudFront.

Code (Apache-2.0): https://github.com/UgaTheDev/your-own-commentator

Translations of the non-English commentary in the video (the same two-pointer, called by each commentator):
- Spanish (Novato): "El equipo rosa anota una canasta de 2 puntos, ahora el marcador es 13 a 6." = "The pink team scores a 2-point basket; the score is now 13 to 6."
- Hindi: "पिंक टीम ने दो पॉइंट स्कोर किए हैं, स्कोर अब पिंक १३ और ब्लू ६ है।" = "The pink team scored two points; the score is now pink 13, blue 6."

Game footage: Hardwick Community Television (HCTV), "Girls Varsity Basketball v. Lake Region - February 11, 2026", CC BY 4.0, via Wikimedia Commons. Original audio removed; commentary and crowd ambience added. Crowd ambience: SSE Library, CC0. Players are referred to only by team colour and jersey number.
